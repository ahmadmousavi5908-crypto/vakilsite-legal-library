# Changelog

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-01
- Added source-review findings for the phased launch of the Article 10 claim-registration system.
- Clarified that Article 1 consequences must be assessed by property document date and the officially announced launch scope.
- Added summaries for Article 10 of the compulsory real-estate registration law and Articles 4 and 21 bis of the Cheque Issuance Law.
- Recorded the 1400/01/29 cheque-law amendment affecting the Sayad registration and transfer rules.
- Linked cheque and unregistered-property terms to the additional legal provisions.
- Kept legal statuses at secondary-source level; no blanket primary-source consolidation claim is made.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-01
- Created an independent, data-only Iranian legal research library.
- Prioritized real-estate registration and property terminology.
- Included cheque, Sayad cheque and promissory-note terminology in v1.
- Added civil law, civil procedure, criminal law, laws, article summaries, taxonomy and relations.
- Added a Python standard-library validator suitable for Termux.
- Marked records for legal review where primary-source consolidation is incomplete.
